# Boss artwork

Blizzard Entertainment artwork, downloaded for the guild dashboard.

- Achievement icons: https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/large/
- Encounter Journal portraits: https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-textures/tree/live/EncounterJournal
- Source mappings and conversion: `scripts/fetch-boss-icons.py` (Python + Pillow).

Images are bundled as WebP; browsers make no third-party image requests.
Council/group encounters use a representative icon. Toravon and the Ruby Sanctum
lieutenants use themed frost/dragon icons where dedicated portraits are unavailable.
